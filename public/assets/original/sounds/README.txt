Escape Velocity (EV) Sound Effects Directory
=============================================
This directory contains the audio sound effects for The Cosmos: Derelict's Hub.
You can replace any of the following sound files with your original audio files
(in .wav, .mp3, or .ogg formats):

- select.wav      : Item selection & list focus click
- menu.wav        : Navigation menus & sub-tab switches
- warp.wav        : Hyperspace jump & warp travel whoosh
- land.wav        : Atmospheric planetary landing & station docking clamp
- credit.wav      : Cargo transactions, trading, loans, payouts
- alert.wav       : Tactical threat warnings & combat encounter alarms
- fuel.wav        : Fuel tank refueling chime
- error.wav       : Prohibited action / out-of-fuel tone

The sound engine will automatically load and play files from this directory.
If an audio file is unavailable or unsupported by the browser, the Web Audio
procedural synthesizer will seamlessly provide authentic retro audio as a fallback.
